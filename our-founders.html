Welcome to an Institute where profession is not just a profession but a lifestyle; where we transform students to become a showman, a personality with unparalleled intelligence and wisdom, who never gives up this positive attitude, a strong "can do" approach. NIMAS, awarding degree in various undergraduate and postgraduate disciplines offered by MAULANA ABUL KALAM AZAD UNIVERSITY OF TECHNOLOGY (formerly WEST BENGAL UNIVERSITY OF TECHNOLOGY). Our aim is to prepare students for a top career in the service industries. By balancing theory with practice and independent learning with strong faculty support, we give students the skills to launch brilliant and successful careers. The institute is fully equipped with appropriate laboratories, spacious classrooms, library for the students to acquire the required standards in degree level program. Faculties are experienced & qualified who specialize in dissemination of knowledge in their respective field of expertise. NIMAS always inspires students to take action and encourage them to do something positive by changing the way they look at things.

NIMAS, with its degree programs, offers quality education on different educational levels. All programs have a consistent training approach where individual development of each student is the focus.

Our curriculum is designed to develop the competencies needed – at the highest level of quality – to enable our graduates to become true experts and professionals in the hospitality and tourism industry. In order to keep up-to-date with new market demands, NIMAS collaborates closely with various national and international industry representatives.

NIMAS's unique teaching pedagogy prepares you for a career in a wide variety of hospitality, management and IT businesses. It also equips you with the necessary business acumen to work in a number of other professions outside the industry. The balance of theory and real industry placements provides graduates with a genuine advantage when competing for future employment.

NIMAS graduates are working globally as supervisors and specialists right through to senior and general managers. A number also work outside the industry in areas such as marketing, IT, human resources and their own businesses
