B. Optometry

The undergraduate programme B. Optom. is a four-year UGC recognised degree course under Maulana Abul Kalam Azad University of Technology (MAKAUT).

Optometry is a health care profession which deals with the examination, diagnosis, treatment and management of diseases and disorders of the visual system. It is a vision care science. One can also define it as the science of eye equipment (including lenses and spectacles) which is imbued with the idea of improving the vision of the human eye and remove all kinds of obstacles of sight which an individual may experience. This is a dynamic and challenging career, which allows one to help people, achieve personal growth, community respect, job flexibility and financial success and offers virtually unlimited opportunities.

Eligibility Criteria
Curriculum
Duration

B. Optom. programme at NIMAS is unique in its objective to

  • Develop a multipurpose ophthalmic manpower at paramedical level
  • Enable a student to become a competent person in providing service as an Optician, Optometrist, Refractionist and Ophthalmic Assistant to the community in urban, semi-urban and rural settings in private, semi-Governmental and Governmental sectors

Eligibility Criteria

Passed 10+2 examination from a recognized board with Physics, Chemistry, and Biology/Mathematics with minimum qualifying marks (preferred 50% aggregate).

Core Curriculum Focus

Comprehensive studies in Ocular Anatomy, Geometric Optics, Visual Optics, Optometric Instruments, Contact Lenses, Binocular Vision, and Clinical Optometry.

Program Duration

4 Years (including mandatory clinical internship and hospital training).
