MAKAUT WB CET Entrance Exam

MAKAUT WB CET (MAKAUT Common Entrance Test) is the State level UG and PG entrance exam. MAKAUT WB CET exam mode is an Offline mode conducted by Maulana Abul Kalam Azad University of Technology. MAKAUT WB CET is a UG and PG entrance exam conducted for admission to MAKAUT and Non AICTE Institutes. MAKAUT WB CET exam is conducted for admission to B.Sc, BBA, BCA programs.
