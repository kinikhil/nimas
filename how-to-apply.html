How to Apply

STEP 1

Check Your Eligibility

  • Passed or appeared in Higher Secondary/Graduation or equivalent examination with required subject combinations.
  • Students who are due to appear for Class XII final exams/awaiting results may be given provisional admission depending on their Personal Interview (PI) and CET score.
  • Admissions are strictly on a merit basis.

STEP 3

Enquire at Our Admission Help-Desk

  • Enquiry Forms can be obtained, filled, and submitted at the NIMAS campus.
  • You can submit your queries online directly through the website.
  • Use our Live Chat option on the website to ask queries and interact with admission support.
  • Post-enquiry, you can avail a free counseling session with our counselors.
  • All your queries will be answered via phone call, mail, or face-to-face interaction.
STEP 4

Documentation & Application Fee

After obtaining all the required information for a specific course, fill and submit the admission form with a sum of Rs. 700 directly at the campus.

Payment Methods:

  • Directly at the campus via Cash or DD payable at NIMAS, Kolkata.
  • Online Payment via NEFT / IMPS / RTGS.
  • Post-dated Cheques (PDCs) for all installments to be submitted at the time of admission.

Documents required at the time of Application (Submit to campus counselors, not online):

  • Self-attested copy of Class X and Class XII admit cards.
  • Self-attested copy of Class X and Class XII mark sheets.
  • One passport-size photograph with self-attested copy of birth certificate.
  • Self-copies of passport/visa in case of foreign students.
  • School Leaving Certificate or Migration Certificate.
STEP 5

Registration & Admissions

  • Selected candidates will be notified about their registration date and time via email and/or phone call.
  • Pay the registration fee and collect the registration letter from the campus.

Documents required at the time of Admission (duly signed/attested by Grade-1 Officer):

  • Class X Mark Sheet and Certificate (2 copies)
  • Class X Admit Card (2 copies)
  • Class XII Mark Sheet and Certificate (2 copies)
  • Class XII Admit Card (2 copies)
  • Migration Certificate & School Leaving Certificate (2 copies)
  • Passport-sized photographs (6 copies)
  • Caste Certificate for other than general candidates (2 copies)
  • Address Proof (2 copies)
