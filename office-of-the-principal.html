Dr. Swapan Kumar Kar

Principal, NIMAS

At our institution, education is viewed as a transformative life journey rather than just a career path. We aim to nurture well-rounded individuals equipped with keen intelligence, strong wisdom, and a resilient, positive attitude towards every challenge.

Our primary focus is to prepare students for rewarding leadership careers in service industries. By blending solid theoretical knowledge with practical hands-on experience, and balancing independent learning with faculty support, we give our students the tools to launch successful professional careers. The campus features modern laboratories, spacious classrooms, and an extensive library designed to meet degree-level standards. Our experienced faculty specialize in imparting knowledge effectively while inspiring students to adopt proactive, innovative perspectives.

NIMAS offers quality degree programs across various academic levels, structured around a consistent training methodology that prioritizes the personal and professional growth of every student.

Throughout their academic semesters, students gain comprehensive insights into the global hospitality and management sectors—ranging from fundamental operations to high-level strategic management within broader socio-economic frameworks.

Students participate in real-world industry internships across India, providing hands-on experience beyond the classroom. This practical exposure helps solidify theoretical concepts learned during lectures while enabling students to build valuable industry networks early in their careers.

Our alumni work worldwide in diverse roles, from supervisory and specialist positions to general management roles. Many also excel outside the immediate field in domains such as marketing, IT, human resources, and entrepreneurship.

Every program and facility at NIMAS is crafted to equip students with both academic foundation and practical competence to thrive in their chosen professions.

Wishing you great success in all your future endeavors.
