Campus Overview & Location

The MAKAUT affiliated NIMAS Campus is situated at the outskirts of Kolkata[cite: 1]. Spread over a lush green campus, the campus proposes dedicated hostel facilities for students, dedicated hospitality, information technology & management education wings, sports and recreational facilities along with conferencing and commercial facilities[cite: 1]. Built to integrate 'innovation' and 'career' within a single environment, the campus has ultra-modern amenities for all students[cite: 1].

The Campus landscape is and has always been central to the identity of NIMAS Barasat and to the experience of its students[cite: 1]. The Campus has a marked individual character not found in too many Institutes in West Bengal[cite: 1]. Students have the freedom of studying in a midst of intricate beauty and greenery spread over 0.5 acres and surrounded by picturesque landscape[cite: 1].

Situated in the Barrackpore Barasat Road (Behind Lokenath Mandir) it is well connected by Bus or Train[cite: 1]. Kolkata to Barasat - 1 hr 15 min / 23 km[cite: 1].
