M.Sc. in Clinical Psychology

Course Description

M.Sc. in Clinical Psychology is a 2-year full-time post graduate course divided across 4 semesters. It has been designed to develop ethical knowledge on Clinical Psychology.

Program Overview

This 2-yr PG-programme on MSc. in Clinical Psychology will provide ethical knowledge on Clinical Psychology. The course has an interdisciplinary and holistic academic approach. After completion of this course the students will achieve the required skills and competencies required by the industry to function as a professional. The expertise gained from this course will enable the student to function as a professional in various inter-related grounds of operation that includes not only the field of Psychology, but also at the larger plane of the Health Sector, Academic Sector, in the HR division of the Corporate Sector. It will nurture the students towards academic interest in Clinical Psychology along with their personal growth of their own personality and awareness towards the development of society at large. It will provide further interest in the student for higher research. The Specialization in the area of Psychodynamic Orientation in Clinical Psychology is most unique in the country, incorporated within the syllabus of an M.Sc. programme in Clinical Psychology.

Eligibility Criteria

Bachelor's degree (any stream) from a recognised university.

Curriculum (Course Modules)

  • Cognitive Psychology
  • Physiological Psychology
  • Theories of Personality Structure
  • Application of Psychology in Community
  • Developmental Psychology
  • Fundamentals of Clinical Psychology
  • Research methodology
  • Statistics in Psychology & Scale Construction
  • Advanced Psychopathology
  • Childhood & Developmental Disorders
  • Psychotherapy & Counseling
  • Psychotherapy Processes
  • Clinical Assessment & Intervention
  • Psychotherapy
  • Health Psychology
