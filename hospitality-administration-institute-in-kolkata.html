B.Sc. in Hospitality & Hotel Administration (BHHA)

Course Description

The programme exposes the student to the diversity of hospitality industry and equips the student with intellectual & leadership capabilities allowing the student to move into diverse managerial roles in prestigious international organizations. Its multi-disciplinary programme focuses on critical analysis of management styles & strategic thinking required to succeed in hotel management.

Program Overview

Create a blue print to your hospitality career & get exposed to crucial hospitality management concepts. Understand the fundamentals of the Hotel Industry, Commercial Kitchen Operations, Food & Beverage Service and the Wines. Introduction to key principles of Human Resources, Customer Centered Marketing and specific Finance & Accounting for Hotel industry. Focus on delivering superior performance and value to your customer.

Eligibility Criteria

Passed or appeared in Higher Secondary or equivalent examination (CBSE, ISC, A Levels or any other equivalent Board examination) with English as a compulsory subject are preferred. Students who are due to appear for Class XII final exams/awaiting results may be given provisional admission depending on their CET score. Admissions are strictly on merit basis.

Curriculum (Course Modules)

FIRST YEAR: Food Production & Patisserie-I, Food & Beverage Service -I, Front Office Operations, Accommodation Operation, Computer Fundamentals, Food Costing, Nutrition & Food Science, Hotel French I, Introduction to Accounting, English Communication, Food Production & Patisserie-II, Food & Beverage Service -II.

SECOND YEAR: Food Production, Food and Beverage Service, Front Office Operations, Accommodation Operation, Hotel Engineering, Hotel Accountancy, Hotel and Business Law, Business Communication, English Communication, Industrial Training (6 Months), Project Work.

THIRD YEAR: Advanced Food Production & Patisserie, HR Management & Hotel Laws, Financial Management, Facility Planning, F & B Management, Sales and Marketing, English Communication, Research Projects.

Duration

3 Years- Full Time
