The Hands That Built a Future

Founders of the Institute

This commemorative layout presents the story of two founding pillars of NIMAS—Sri Sudhir Kumar Bhattacharya and Smt. Gouri Devi—whose belief in education as a path to dignity, growth, and opportunity helped shape generations of young learners. Their enduring contribution lies not only in starting an institute, but in nurturing it with patience, values, and a deep commitment to student advancement.

Founding Vision

The institute was envisioned as a place where ambition could meet guidance, and where students from varied backgrounds could discover meaningful avenues for academic progress, skill development, and a more secure future.

What began with limited means was strengthened through tireless personal involvement, compassionate leadership, and an unwavering belief that each student deserved careful mentoring and a chance to rise.

Sri Sudhir Kumar Bhattacharya

A guiding force in establishing the academic identity and social purpose of NIMAS.

Sri Sudhir Kumar Bhattacharya is remembered as a foundational architect of NIMAS—someone whose vision reached beyond the creation of an institute and extended toward the making of opportunity for the younger generation.

At a time when many students required not only education but also direction, encouragement, and practical pathways to advancement, he recognized the urgent need for an institution that could combine aspiration with accessibility. His contribution lay in giving shape to that need: building a place where students could prepare themselves for meaningful growth, stronger futures, and greater confidence in the possibilities before them.

“He helped plant the institution on values of discipline, purpose, and genuine concern for student progress.”

The rise of the institute from modest beginnings demanded more than administration—it required personal dedication, faith in the mission, and close involvement in the early years when every step mattered. Sri Bhattacharya is imagined here as one who hand-held the development of the institute with steadiness and resolve, helping transform limited resources into a credible educational foundation.

Vision

Envisioned an institute that could serve students seeking direction, capability, and a secure path toward future success.

Foundation

Helped establish the early academic and institutional framework with an emphasis on sincerity, standards, and service.

Guidance

Encouraged a culture where learners were mentored with care, discipline, and belief in their long-term potential.

Growth

Supported the institute through its formative phase, helping it evolve into a name associated with trust and repute.

Smt. Gouri Devi

A nurturing pillar whose care, resilience, and quiet strength sustained the institute’s formative journey.

Smt. Gouri Devi stands as a symbol of the warmth, perseverance, and human concern that often lies behind great institutions. Her contribution to the founding spirit of NIMAS can be understood as deeply personal and profoundly sustaining.

Where an institution requires structure, it also needs compassion—and in this vision of NIMAS’s beginnings, she represents the nurturing force that helped the institute remain centered on people, especially students stepping toward uncertain futures. Her role reflects an abiding belief that education should do more than instruct; it should reassure, uplift, and open avenues for a better life.

“She helped preserve the institution’s heart—ensuring that growth never outpaced care.”

As the institute rose from humble origins, Smt. Gouri Devi’s support is remembered as integral to that journey. Through steadiness, sacrifice, and moral encouragement, she helped sustain the founding mission: to guide young learners with empathy, to stand beside them in their aspirations, and to help transform a modest initiative into an institution of respect and relevance.

Care

Embodied the compassionate ethos of the institute, keeping student welfare central to its educational purpose.

Support

Strengthened the founders’ mission during the early years through resilience, encouragement, and steadfast involvement.

Values

Helped shape an environment rooted in sincerity, dignity, inclusiveness, and hope for every learner’s future.

Legacy

Contributed to the transformation of a modest beginning into an institution known for guidance, trust, and educational aspiration.
